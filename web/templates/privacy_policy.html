ACP Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

ACP is a social media management application that lets an authorized user connect a Threads account and publish content that the user chooses to publish.

Information ACP receives

When you connect Threads through Meta's authorization flow, ACP may receive the Threads account identifier, username and profile information made available by the permissions you approve. ACP also receives an access token needed to access the Threads API on your behalf.

When you use publishing features, ACP processes the text, media references and publishing instructions that you explicitly provide to the application.

How ACP uses this information

ACP uses Threads account data only to identify the connected account, maintain the authorized connection, and perform actions that the user requests, including publishing Threads content. ACP does not use Threads data to build advertising profiles and does not sell Threads account data.

Access tokens and security

Threads access tokens are treated as credentials. ACP stores connected-account access tokens in encrypted form and does not intentionally expose them in the user interface or application logs.

Sharing

ACP sends data to Meta/Threads when required to complete authorization or to perform a user-requested Threads action. ACP does not sell Threads data. Data may also be processed by infrastructure providers that are necessary to operate the deployment, if such providers are configured by the ACP operator.

Retention and deletion

ACP retains connected-account information only for as long as it is needed to provide the requested integration or to meet legitimate operational requirements. You may request deletion of your ACP connection data and stored access credentials at any time.

Deletion instructions are available at ACP Data Deletion.

Your choices

You control whether to authorize ACP through the Meta/Threads authorization window. You may stop using the integration and request deletion of stored connection data.
